## Filled-in Templates (Examples)

Use the template to build targeted prompts:
> Act as [PERSONA] ...... for [AUDIENCE]. The current issue is [SITUATION]. I intend to [INTENT].
> Explain the core ideas of [CONCEPT] with/without using [RESTRICTIONS].
 
### Example 1: Police Officer
**Prompt:**
> Act as a police officer providing crime prevention guidance and community safety updates for local business owners, merchants, retail employees, and neighborhood residents.
> A local business district is experiencing a sudden spike in nighttime property thefts, creating unease among merchants. The department needs to deter further burglaries and reassure the community before panic spreads.
> I intend to reduce nighttime burglaries through targeted patrols and build stronger community trust to alleviate business owners' safety concerns. Focus on community-oriented policing, situational awareness, and proactive crime prevention.
> Keep strategies compliant with local municipal policies, privacy laws, and department resource caps.

### Example 2: Teacher
**Prompt:**
> Act as a teacher providing instructional explanations and practical learning support for eighth-grade students struggling with foundational math concepts, along with their parents or guardians.
> The current issue is that a significant portion of the eighth-grade math class failed the recent fractions unit assessment, indicating a widespread gap in foundational understanding, and the instructor must adjust the upcoming lesson plans to address these learning deficits before moving forward.
> I intend to re-teach core concepts so that students improve their comprehension and pass the upcoming remedial assessments before advancing the curriculum.
> Apply formative assessment, differentiated instruction, and scaffolding techniques. All recommendations must align with standardized educational criteria and fit within a standard 50-minute class period.

### Example 3: Nurse
**Prompt:**
> Act as a nurse delivering clear discharge instructions, triage updates, and care communication for patients, their families, and the multidisciplinary healthcare team (doctors, techs, and fellow nursing staff).
> The emergency department is currently at maximum capacity. The nurse must rapidly triage incoming patients and reallocate available resources to maintain safe care standards.
> I intend to safely manage a high-capacity emergency department during a severe staff shortage.
> Base decisions on crisis resource management and workload balancing.
> Ensure strict adherence to HIPAA privacy guidelines and hospital regulatory compliance standards under emergency conditions.

## Quick Check
Before moving to the next step, make sure you can answer these questions:

- [ ] **Persona:** Whom should the AI act as?
- [ ] **Audience:** Who is the response for?
- [ ] **Situation:** What is the background context or problem?
- [ ] **Intent:** Why you're asking and what you plan to do with the information.
- [ ] **Concept:** Which core topics must prompt be included?
- [ ] **Restrictions:** What rules, limits, or exclusions must the AI follow?
