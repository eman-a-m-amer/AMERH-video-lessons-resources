## 3. Situation (What is the current state?)
Specify the context and problem the request is addressing.

### Examples:
- **(Police Officer):** A local business district is experiencing a sudden spike in nighttime property thefts, creating unease among merchants. The department needs to deter further burglaries and reassure the community before panic spreads.

- **(Teacher):** A significant portion of the eighth-grade math class failed the recent fractions unit assessment, indicating a widespread gap in foundational understanding. The instructor must adjust the upcoming lesson plans to address these learning deficits before moving forward.
- **(Nurse):** The emergency department is currently at maximum capacity. The nurse must rapidly triage incoming patients and reallocate available resources to maintain safe care standards.

## 4. Intent (What do you want to accomplish?)
Specify the primary goal, purpose, or intended outcome of the response.

### Examples:
- **(Police Officer):** Reduce nighttime burglaries through targeted patrols and build stronger community trust to alleviate business owners' safety concerns.

- **(Teacher):** Re-teach core concepts so that students improve their comprehension and pass the upcoming remedial assessments before advancing the curriculum.

- **(Nurse):** Safely manage a high-capacity emergency department during a severe staff shortage.

## Filled-in Templates (Examples)

Use the template to build targeted prompts:
> Act as [PERSONA] ...... for [AUDIENCE]. The current issue is [SITUATION]. I intend to .. [INTENT].
 
### Example 1: Police Officer
**Prompt:**
> Act as a police officer providing crime prevention guidance and community safety updates for local business owners, merchants, retail employees, and neighborhood residents. A local business district is experiencing a sudden spike in nighttime property thefts, creating unease among merchants. The department needs to deter further burglaries and reassure the community before panic spreads. I intend to reduce nighttime burglaries through targeted patrols and build stronger community trust to alleviate business owners' safety concerns.

### Example 2: Teacher
**Prompt:**
> Act as a teacher providing instructional explanations and practical learning support for eighth-grade students struggling with foundational math concepts, along with their parents or guardians. A significant portion of the eighth-grade math class failed the recent fractions unit assessment, indicating a widespread gap in foundational understanding. The instructor must adjust the upcoming lesson plans to address these learning deficits before moving forward. I intend to re-teach core concepts so that students improve their comprehension and pass the upcoming remedial assessments before advancing the curriculum.

### Example 3: Nurse
**Prompt:**
> Act as a nurse delivering clear discharge instructions, triage updates, and care communication for patients, their families, and the multidisciplinary healthcare team (doctors, techs, and fellow nursing staff). The emergency department is currently at maximum capacity. The nurse must rapidly triage incoming patients and reallocate available resources to maintain safe care standards. I intend to safely manage a high-capacity emergency department during a severe staff shortage. 


## Quick Check
Before moving to the next step, make sure you can answer these questions:

- [ ] **Persona:** Whom should the AI act as?
- [ ] **Audience:** Who is the response for?
- [ ] **Situation:** What is the background context or problem?
- [ ] **Intent:** Why you're asking and what you plan to do with the information.


>*If all answers are clear, your prompt has a stronger foundation, but we are not done yet.*
